## Overall: CALIBRATED

Agreement ≥ 90% and no probe divergence. Caveats worth knowing:
- **Scores are a weaker signal than verdicts.** Single-defect FAILs often score 0.75–0.82 (one
  REQUIRED dimension at ~0 averaged with clean ones). The `/wf-eval` gate's PASS→FAIL check is
  well-grounded; its "score drop > 0.15" check is noisier — the same ambiguous-spec plan scored 0.76
  and 0.88 on two judge runs today.
- **Narrow domain.** Every artifact is from the toy math-utils fixtures. Agreement here says the judge
  applies these rubrics faithfully; it says less about rubrics for real repos.
